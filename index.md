### Checklist costeiro

- [ ] Identificar o serviço ou evento exato, sem confundir nomes semelhantes.
- [ ] Localizar publicação original, data da informação e órgão responsável.
- [ ] Confirmar dia, endereço, funcionamento e possíveis cancelamentos.
- [ ] Conferir condições de acesso relevantes, sem presumir acessibilidade.
- [ ] Confirmar preços e meios de pagamento diretamente, quando aplicável.
- [ ] Planejar transporte, condições costeiras e alternativa se a informação continuar desconhecida.

## EN

### From an idea to confirmation

This directory links official portals as research starting points. A municipal link does not verify a market, address, service, opening time or access condition. There are no interactive filters, calculator, bookings, commercial listings or real-time operational records. Confirm directly with the responsible source before travelling.

The institutional directory above covers Santos, Guarujá and São Vicente. For every entry, the particular event/service, date, address, hours, access and contact remain unverified. No schedule was collected or venue inspected. Missing entries do not imply a city has no services; inclusion is neither a commercial endorsement nor official affiliation.

### Coastal checklist

- [ ] Identify the particular service or event without confusing similar names.
- [ ] Find the original notice, its date and the responsible authority.
- [ ] Confirm day, address, operating hours and cancellations.
- [ ] Check relevant access needs without assuming accessibility.
- [ ] Confirm prices and payment methods directly where applicable.
- [ ] Plan transport, coastal conditions and an alternative if facts remain unknown.
